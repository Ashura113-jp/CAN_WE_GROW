10 small games to explore

◌Calm · Tense

Breath Garden

Follow a soft expanding circle for five unhurried breaths. Go at your own pace; stopping is always okay.

●Playful · Focused

Color Current

Find the color that matches the tide. Five rounds, no timer, and a little room to settle in.

▦Focused · Low energy

Pocket Pairs

Turn over two tiles at a time and find four matching pairs in this quiet memory game.

AaFocused · Playful

Word Tangle

Untangle five friendly little words. Type your answer, or use the hint when you want one.

♫Playful · Focused

Echo Garden

Listen with your eyes, then repeat a growing sequence of garden notes. There’s no rush.

⌁Focused · Low energy

Little Maze

Guide a tiny star through a pocket-sized maze. Use the arrows, WASD, or the on-screen buttons.

☁Low energy · A little alone

Cloud Match

Pick the cloud that matches the little shape above it. A soft, simple game for a quiet minute.

✎A little alone · Playful

Story Sprout

Choose a few words and grow a tiny story. Keep it private, share it, or leave it unfinished.

⌕Focused · Tense

Find the Odd One

Spot the one symbol that’s different from the rest. Five rounds, at whatever speed feels good.

✧Celebrating · Focused

Pattern Lanterns

Work out which lantern completes each little pattern, and light up the path one round at a time.
